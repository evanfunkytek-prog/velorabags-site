Compliance

AGEC, Triman and French Labelling

France ties packaging, sorting information and consumer language together under one law.

  • AGEC and EPR data — material composition and weights per SKU for your scheme reporting.
  • Triman mark placement built into the print layout when you supply the sorting instruction that applies.
  • French-language consumer information — we reserve space in the artwork so your French text fits without redesign.
  • Material marking for the fabric and trim so sorting instructions match the actual construction.
  • REACH and OEKO-TEX documentation for the materials in your bill of materials.
  • Recycled content options with GRS certificates where you are making a recycled claim.
Logistics

Getting the Goods to France

Sea freight

Ningbo or Shenzhen into Le Havre, Fos-sur-Mer and Marseille, then onward by road. Typically 30–38 days port to door.

Rail

Corridor services terminate in Duisburg and Lyon, both practical for onward French distribution on larger runs.

Air

7–10 days door to door via Paris CDG for launch stock and high-value accessories.

Terms

DDP to your French warehouse, or DAP and FOB if you prefer your own customs broker.
