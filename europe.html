Import paperwork

The Documentation Pack We Prepare

Your customs broker and your compliance team both need data from the supplier. We prepare it at order stage rather than on request.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued in the format your broker expects, with your EORI reference.
  • Correct HS codes for tote, shopping, promotional and insulated bags so duty is assessed properly.
  • Material composition and weights per SKU, the input your EPR registration depends on.
  • REACH and OEKO-TEX documentation for fabric, webbing, lining and trims.
  • Recycled-content evidence with GRS transaction certificates where recycled material is used.
  • Carton data including weights, dimensions, polybag material and units per carton.
