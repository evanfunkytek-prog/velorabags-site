Materials & construction

Choose the right base for the programme

Use the material language that matches the actual construction: write PU leather for a full PU outer shell, PU-trimmed for PU panels or trim, and canvas or linen when those are the confirmed shell materials.

PU leather

Full PU outer shells are available for structured handbags. Specify surface finish, lining, closure and hardware together during sampling.

Canvas and linen

Coated canvas, washed cotton and linen or cotton-linen constructions support softer totes, lifestyle collections and promotional programmes.

Branding and hardware

Specify metal logo plates, embroidery, foil stamping, patches, straps, magnetic closures, zips and custom packaging.

Questions

Custom handbag FAQ

Standard styles start from 100 pcs. Custom orders start from 300 pcs, depending on the construction and materials.
Standard-style samples are free. Custom samples are USD 60–150 depending on the programme. The sample fee is refunded when the order is placed or when cumulative quantity reaches 1,000 pcs.
Sampling takes 3–5 days after artwork approval. Bulk production takes 15–25 days after sample approval.
