2026 Trends: Why Recycled Materials are Dominating Reusable Bags

Sep 05, 2026 • Bag Industry Insight

The Shift to Circularity

The global ban on single-use plastics is no longer news—the new headline is Circularity. Brands are moving beyond 'reusable' and demanding 'recycled' and 'recyclable'.

RPET: From Bottles to Bags

Recycled Polyethylene Terephthalate (RPET) is seeing a 40% surge in demand. It allows brands to tell a story of waste reduction while maintaining the durability of virgin polyester.

Water-Based Inks

Sustainability isn't just about the fabric; it's about the chemistry. We are transitioning our silk-screen lines to water-based inks which reduce VOC emissions and offer a softer hand-feel on canvas totes.

Minimalist Packaging

The trend is moving towards shipping bags in bulk without individual polybags, or using compostable starch-based sleeves, further reducing the environmental footprint of custom programs.
