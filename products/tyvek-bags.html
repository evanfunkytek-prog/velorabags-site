Customisation

Built around your spec sheet

Tyvek-style HDPE fibre behaves like paper but resists water and tearing. It folds flat for shipping and accepts dense full-colour print.

Material & construction

1073D Tyvek-style HDPE fibre, 60-110 gsm. Soft-touch natural, semi-gloss or full-colour printed.

Branding & hardware

Flexo print, screen print, digital full-colour. Die-cut, cotton rope, webbing or reinforced paper twist.

Order & logistics

MOQ 300 pcs per style. Lead time 15-25 days after sample approval. Polybag and export carton packing as standard.
