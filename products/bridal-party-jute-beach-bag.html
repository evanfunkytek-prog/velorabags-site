Customisation

Built around your spec sheet

Jute is the workhorse of reusable retail packaging: strong, plant-based and photogenic. We laminate, print and stitch it in-house, so your artwork survives the journey from shelf to street.

Material & construction

Laminated jute 240-340 gsm, natural / bleached / dyed. Matte or gloss BOPP lamination; LDPE inner lining optional.

Branding & hardware

Screen print, heat transfer, woven label, leather or cork patch. Cotton tape, padded cotton, jute-cotton rope or die-cut handle.

Order & logistics

MOQ 300 pcs per style. Lead time 15-25 days after sample approval. Polybag and export carton packing as standard.
