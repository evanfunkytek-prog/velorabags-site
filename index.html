How it works

From Your Idea to Container, in Four Steps

Inquiry & Brief

Share your reference, sketch or spec. We reply within one business day with a cost range and MOQ.

Design & Sample

Pattern engineers develop your style, then we sew 1–3 samples until approval on material and stitch.

Bulk Production

Approved sample locked. Pre-production meeting, cutting, sewing, assembly and inline QA on the line.

QC & Shipment

100% inspection checklist, drop and zipper-cycle tests, then consolidated packing for sea or air freight.
